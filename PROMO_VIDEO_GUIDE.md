## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Recording & Production Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Your Recording Environment
1. **Clean Browser Profile**:
   - Open a fresh Chrome window (no personal bookmarks bar or clutter).
   - Set browser zoom to **100%** or **110%** for clear visibility.
2. **Resolution**:
   - Record at **1920 × 1080 (1080p)** or **2560 × 1440 (1440p)** at **60 FPS** using **OBS Studio**, **QuickTime**, or **Screen Studio**.
3. **Mouse Pointer**:
   - Use a clear, standard cursor (or enable subtle click ripple highlights in Screen Studio / Camtasia).

---

### Step 2: Record Individual Screen Takes (Do Not Record in 1 Shot)
- **Take 1 (0:00 - 0:08)**: 6 open tabs switching rapidly.
- **Take 2 (0:08 - 0:18)**: Dragging the floating pill widget on a modern website (e.g. Wikipedia, GitHub, or an AI news article) and expanding it.
- **Take 3 (0:18 - 0:32)**: Typing a prompt and watching responses stream in across providers.
- **Take 4 (0:32 - 0:44)**: Typing `@area`, selecting a chart, and asking a query.
- **Take 5 (0:44 - 0:54)**: Clicking the Scan OCR icon, selecting text in an image, and seeing it populate the prompt.
- **Take 6 (0:54 - 0:65)**: Clicking through Settings, changing themes (Dark/Solid/Glass), and showing the `?` Guide tab.
- **Take 7 (0:65 - 0:75)**: Clicking History and showing end title card.

---

### Step 3: Video Editing & Polishing (CapCut, Premiere, DaVinci, or Canva)
1. **Smooth Zooms**: Add gentle 1.1x–1.2x digital zoom-ins to UI controls (like the prompt bar, `@` menu, and theme switchers).
2. **Sound Effects (SFX)**:
   - Subtle *whoosh* sound when expanding the widget.
   - Clean *click / pop* sound when selecting tabs or confirming area selection.
   - Soft *chime* sound when OCR text populates.
3. **Animated Text Callouts**: Use clean modern typography (Inter / Outfit / SF Pro) in white and bright blue (`#2563eb`).
4. **Export Settings**:
   - Format: **MP4 (H.264)**
   - Bitrate: **12–16 Mbps**
   - Resolution: **1920 × 1080**
   - Audio: **Stereo 320 kbps AAC**
